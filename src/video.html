What is USDT?

Stay safe

Golden safety rules

Never share your secrets
Password, 2FA codes and recovery phrase stay with you. Always.
Support never asks for them
Anyone asking for your codes or phrase is a scammer.
"Guaranteed profit" = scam
Ignore promises of easy or risk-free returns.
Double-check everything
Check the address and the network before you confirm.
