=== Full message (Discord / chat / email) ===

**How to pay with USDT (MoonPay)**

You can pay with USDT using MoonPay. It takes about 5-10 minutes and no crypto
experience is needed. The video above shows every step.

We will send you the amount and the wallet address for your payment.

**How to pay:**
1. Go to moonpay.com or download the official MoonPay app, and sign up with
   your email.
2. First time only: verify your identity (a few personal details, a photo of
   your ID and a quick selfie).
3. Choose **Tether (USDT)** on the **BNB Smart Chain (BEP-20)** network.
   Make sure the "You get" amount is at least the USDT amount we asked for,
   because MoonPay's fees come out of what you pay.
4. When MoonPay asks for a wallet address, **paste** the address we sent you
   (don't type it). Don't create a new wallet at checkout.
5. Pay with your card, Apple Pay, Google Pay or PayPal, and confirm.

**Before you confirm, please check:**
- The network is BNB Smart Chain (BEP-20)
- The address starts with 0x, and its first and last characters match the
  address we sent you

**Good to know:**
- We give a new wallet address for each payment, so always use the latest
  address we sent you.
- If your card is declined, add money to your MoonPay Balance first, then buy
  from your balance.
- Once you've paid, send us a screenshot of the confirmation so we can confirm
  your payment quickly.
